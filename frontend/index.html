    ⬡ About Simly Orbis

    Simly Orbis is a simulated world for testing before building. It gives students, scientists, and engineers a sandbox where actions carry physical consequences and mathematical rigor before touching physical equipment or real patients.

    🔬 What is Real:

    • Exact Bateman two-compartment superposition pharmacokinetics differential equations.
    • Volumetric acid-base titration equivalence equilibrium curves with Henderson-Hasselbalch indicator inflection.
    • Monte Carlo population variation with log-normal clearance distributions and analytical reality check invariants.
    • Deterministic safety rules engine with explicit severity penalties and educational feedback.

    ⚖️ What is Simplified:

    • Patient vitals (heart rate and SpO2) are illustrative heuristics designed for clinical game feedback.
    • Laboratory apparatus interactions are streamlined to protocol essentials (weigh, dissolve, pipette, titrate).
    • Time progression is quantized for educational walkthroughs.

    🚀 Upcoming Simulation Districts:

    Orbital Mechanics: Keplerian two-body celestial orbital dynamics, Hohmann transfer windows, and delta-v budgets.

    Market Microstructure: Continuous double auctions, limit order books, and multi-agent liquidity equilibrium.

    Educational simulation. Not medical advice. Not clinical evidence.
