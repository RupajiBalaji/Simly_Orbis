About Simly Orbis

A simulated world for testing before building.

🌍 The Big Vision

Complex systems in medicine, biotechnology, engineering, and aerospace are risky and costly to test on real-world subjects. Simly Orbis is conceived as a universal simulation sandbox — a virtual playground that models biological, physical, and biochemical dynamics accurately enough to test hypotheses, strategies, and interventions before committing real resources or risking real lives.

🧪 First Sample: Pharmacokinetics (PK)

This application represents our first interactive slice: an oral pharmacokinetics simulation engine. Using the classical one-compartment Bateman absorption model with linear superposition for multiple dosing, students and residents can experiment with drug kinetics, organ impairment (e.g. reduced hepatic clearance), body weight shifts, and dose scheduling in real time.

PK Model

One-compartment Bateman equation (oral absorption):

C(t) = F · D · kaVd · (ka − ke) · (e−ket − e−kat)
F = bioavailability D = dose (mg) ka = absorption rate (h⁻¹) ke = ln 2 / t½ · clearance multiplier Vd = Vd/kg · weight (L)

Multiple doses use superposition. When ka = ke the limit form C = (F·D·ka/Vd) · t · e−ket is used.
