    ⬡ About Simly Orbis

    A science learning game where you play the scientist, not the spectator.

    Step into a fully interactive 3D laboratory where every dose, dilution, and pipetting action carries physical consequences before touching real equipment or human patients.

    🏗️ Architecture in 4 Bullets:

    • FastAPI Backend: Async Python microservices with token-bucket rate limiting (120/min general, 10/min AI), 20 KB body protection, security headers (CSP, nosniff), and MongoDB Atlas with in-memory fallback.
    • Procedural Three.js 3D Frontend: 100% procedural geometry and custom materials running at 60 FPS on our test laptop (AMD Ryzen, integrated graphics) with zero external 3D model downloads (free demo server may take up to a minute to wake up).
    • Deterministic Simulation Engines: Exact Bateman one-compartment superposition PK equations and a rigorous charge-balance equilibrium solver as the mathematical single source of truth.
    • Ask Orbis & Mentor: AI proposes, the engine decides. Context-aware Google Gemini planner and pedagogical mentor with strict prompt-injection defenses, bounds validation, and deterministic rule-based fallback.

    🎯 Why Deterministic First?

    Simulations must be reproducible, mathematically rigorous, and independently verifiable. In Simly Orbis, AI proposes, the engine decides: the physics engine calculates exact outcomes; the AI explains why phenomena occur, never inventing numbers or overriding physics.

    ✅ Verification & Reality Checks:

    Inspect the in-game Reality Check board on the lab wall to view analytical invariant tests checked against textbook equations and internal consistency; not clinical validation. The engine is safeguarded by 90+ automated pytest test cases in CI.
