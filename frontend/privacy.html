Privacy Notice

Legal Notice: A pilot with minors requires legal review. Before any pilot involving users under 18, the founder must confirm India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requirements with qualified counsel. This product does not claim legal compliance prior to that review.

What We Collect

  • Session records, experiment scores, grades, rule violations, and completion times.
  • Nicknames (filtered to exclude real names, emails, and phone numbers) and hashed class codes.
  • Teacher email (only for invite-only pilot teacher accounts) and AI usage counts.

What We Never Collect

  • No student email addresses, no third-party trackers, and no advertising.
  • No long-term IP addresses, no audio recordings, and no stored AI prompt content by default.

Retention & Deletion

Guest sessions are purged after 90 days (configurable). Teachers can delete a class and every linked record at any time from the Teacher Dashboard. See docs/PRIVACY.md for the full deletion-request procedure.
