# Known Issues & Limitations

> **DISCLAIMER: Educational simulation platform. Not medical advice. Not clinical evidence.**
> Simly Orbis is an interactive training and exploratory educational tool. Mathematical models and simulations are intended for educational demonstration and procedural intuition only.

This document honestly tracks known limitations, unverified model parameters, engine scope boundaries, and technical caveats in the Simly Orbis codebase.

---

| Issue ID | Subsystem | Description & Root Cause | User Impact | Workaround | Severity | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| `ISSUE-001` | Validation / PK | **Validation rows are unverified**: All 22 benchmark rows in `validation/data/*.csv` carry `verified_by = "unverified"`. Preliminary unverified runs show paracetamol $C_{\max}$ under-predicted ~1.8-fold ($4.65\text{ mg/L}$ predicted vs $8.33\text{ mg/L}$ observed) and other metrics within ~2.3-fold error. | The model is educational and unverified, not clinically validated. Headline pass rates display "N/A: awaiting human verification". | Expert/founder must execute source verification worksheet against primary literature. | Medium | Open (Pending Expert Sign-off) |
| `ISSUE-002` | Ask Orbis / Parser | **Fallback parser phrasing coverage**: The deterministic regex/rule-based fallback parser in `backend/app/lab/agent.py` splits compound sentences on conjunctions and punctuation, but does not comprehend all colloquial phrasing, complex nested dependent clauses, or unknown synonyms. Ask Orbis parser corpus (42 phrases) was written against current parser behaviour; first-run 100%; it is a regression suite, not an adversarial test. | Unrecognized clauses are rejected with an explicit notice (`"I did not understand: '...'"`), proposing only understood steps. | Rephrase request using standard lab terminology or use button clicks. | Medium | By Design / Improving |
| `ISSUE-003` | Infrastructure | **Free Render instance spin-down**: Hosted on Render Free Tier (`render.yaml`). Web service hibernates after 15 minutes of inactivity. | Initial connection can take 50–90 seconds while spinning up from cold storage. | Built-in wake-up overlay informs user of cold start progress with automatic retries. | Low | Documented Limitation |
| `ISSUE-004` | Districts / World | **Space unlocked, Market district locked**: `pharma` (Pharmacokinetics Ward), `chemistry` (Titration Bench), and `space` (Launch & Orbit Bench) are fully active. Market Microstructure remains marked "🔒 Coming Soon" in `frontend/index.html`. | Clicking locked Market district card shows a disabled state; users cannot launch Market experiments. | Focus exploration on active Pharma, Chemistry, and Space districts. | Low | Roadmapped |
| `ISSUE-005` | Simulation / Models | **Game-balanced parameters & thresholds**: Scoring thresholds in `backend/app/sim/drugs.json` and rocket ascent loss assumptions in `backend/app/space/orbit.py` ($\Delta v_{\text{loss}} = 1,500\text{ m/s}$) are game-balanced for gameplay feedback and pacing. | Target bands, orbital budgets, and alarms do not represent certified clinical or flight-readiness cutoffs. | Reality Check Board explicitly labels parameters as `game-balanced` vs cited literature. | Medium | Documented Limitation |
| `ISSUE-006` | Chemistry Engine | **Monoprotic closed-system scope**: Titration engine (`backend/app/chem/titration.py:46`) models monoprotic strong/weak acids and strong bases in closed aqueous vessels. Atmospheric $CO_2$ mass transfer and polyprotic acids (e.g. $H_3PO_4$) are out of scope (`validation/TODO_SOURCES.md:34-43`). | Attempting polyprotic titrations will yield unmodeled buffer plateaus. | Use monoprotic analytes (HCl, Acetic acid) per current curriculum. | Low | Roadmapped |
| `ISSUE-007` | Rendering / WebGL | **Software WebGL execution latency**: In headless CI environments or sandboxes without hardware GPU acceleration (e.g., SwiftShader), Playwright rendering runs at reduced FPS and can exceed standard 30s timeouts. | QA runs in software mode take longer and telemetry reports software render metrics. | Make QA timeouts configurable (`QA_TIMEOUT_MS`); label benchmark numbers as software render. | Low | Documented Caveat |
| `ISSUE-008` | Space Engine | **Two-body spherical Earth approximation**: Space engine (`backend/app/space/orbit.py`) models two-body Newtonian orbital mechanics assuming a spherical Earth ($R_\oplus = 6,371\text{ km}$). Oblateness ($J_2$ perturbation), atmospheric drag integration across altitude profiles, and lunar/solar gravitational perturbations are unmodeled. | Orbital elements remain ideal Keplerian ellipses and do not exhibit nodal regression or apsidal precession. | Educational exploration of Tsiolkovsky staging, vis-viva speeds, and Hohmann transfers. | Low | Documented Limitation |
| `ISSUE-009` | Validation / Provenance | **Blank retrieval dates across unverified rows**: All validation reference CSV rows (`pharma_pk_references.csv`, `chem_titration_references.csv`, `space_orbit_references.csv`) have had `retrieved_on` blanked. Previously filled retrieval dates were removed to adhere to the integrity rule that only human verifiers record retrieval dates upon source verification. In addition, space benchmark rows explicitly document the ~0.05% difference between WGS-84 equatorial reference figures and the engine's volumetric spherical radius (6,371 km). | Reference data remains transparently unverified until human audit. | Verifiers fill `retrieved_on` and `verified_by` upon primary literature inspection. | Low | Documented Integrity Gate |
